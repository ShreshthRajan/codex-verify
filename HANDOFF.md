## What the audits found (confirm each)
- **The 300-patch table ran on the wrong patches.** The "Claude patches" numbers ran on SWE-bench's human gold patches, and the tool flags 99% of correct, merged fixes.
- **The "paired" Semgrep baseline ran on different code.**
- **There's no .bib file,** so every citation prints as [?].
- **68.7% accuracy is below the 71.7% of always answering "buggy".**
- **From the earlier audit (in the notes):**
  - 70 of the 99 samples were generated by Claude in one call that wrote both the code and its label, and at least 28 contain literal `# BUG:` comments;
  - the correlation matrix (ρ = 0.05–0.25) behind the independence assumption is never computed anywhere;
  - the χ² against Codex is invalid, because Codex was never run on these samples;
  - the ablation trend is an averaging artifact;
  - the theory mislabels monotonicity as submodularity, and the proof of Theorem 3 asserts P > 1;
  - the reproducibility claims point to things that don't exist.

  The Nov 17 commit `90f9c3b` already removed some unsupported claims and added a paired Semgrep baseline. Check what's left.
